📘 CHEMIST QUEST GUIDE
🗺️ 1. FOLLOW THE MAP
Start at Level 01. Complete a mission to unlock the next level.
⚖️ 2. BALANCE THE EQUATION
Change coefficients only. Never change the chemical formula itself.
H₂ + O₂ → H₂O

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
🔬 3. WATCH THE ATOM LAB
Use the atom counts to check whether the same number of atoms exists on both sides.
❤️ 4. PROTECT YOUR HEARTS
Wrong attempts cost a heart. You have three hearts for each mission.
💡 5. USE HINTS
A hint costs 3 coins and gives a clue without revealing the answer.
⭐ 6. EARN STARS
Stars reward your performance. Faster and cleaner solving earns more stars.
🪙 7. COLLECT COINS
Complete missions to earn coins. Use coins when you need a hint.
